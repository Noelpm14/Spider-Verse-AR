PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 2026

ZERO DATA COLLECTION GUARANTEE: Spider-Verse AR processes all video, hand, and facial tracking 100% locally on your device in your web browser. No camera streams, images, or biometric data are ever recorded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute client-side privacy. When you use this augmented reality web application:

  • Camera Feeds: Your webcam video stays strictly inside your browser's memory and is discarded immediately after each frame renders.
  • Biometric Data: Face and hand landmarks generated by MediaPipe are computed on your device's CPU/GPU and never sent over the network.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.

2. Local Browser Storage

This website uses your browser's localStorage purely to remember your preferences (such as dismissing the privacy consent banner). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Assets

To run high-performance AI models and render typography, your browser requests static public resources from trusted content delivery networks (Google Fonts and jsDelivr for MediaPipe WebAssembly runtimes). These requests only receive standard HTTP headers (such as IP address) required for content transmission.

4. Security

We enforce modern web security practices including Content Security Policies (CSP), secure transport protocols, and client-side isolation to guarantee your camera stream cannot be accessed by unauthorized third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about this privacy policy, you may reach out via the open-source project repository.
